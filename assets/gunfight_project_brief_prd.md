# GUNFIGHT: Project Brief (PRD)

## 1. Project Overview
*   **Project Name:** GUNFIGHT
*   **Platform:** Front-end Web Game
*   **Target Audience:** Junior High School Students (Middle School)
*   **Genre:** Tactical First-Person Shooter (FPS) / Hero Shooter

## 2. Core Gameplay Mechanics
*   **Match Structure:** 5v5 team-based combat.
*   **Session Duration:** 15 minutes per match.
*   **Objective:** Secure victory through tactical combat and objective control.
*   **Controls:** Standard FPS web controls (WASD + Mouse).

## 3. Weapon & Equipment System
*   **Starter Kit:** All players start with a baseline set of three equipment types:
    1.  **Attack (Firearms):** 20 distinct types with varying damage (DMG) and fire rates (FR).
    2.  **Defense (Melee/Armor):** 10 types focused on survivability.
    3.  **Explosives (Bombs):** 5 types for area-of-effect damage.
*   **Progression:** Players earn Combat Credits (CR) based on match performance. CR is used to unlock higher-tier weaponry and equipment.

## 4. Customization & Store
*   **Operator Skins:** 20 distinct tactical outfits (e.g., GHOST_RIDER, TITAN).
*   **Rarity System:** Legendary, Epic, and Rare tiers.
*   **Weapon Skins:** Custom visual designs for each weapon type.
*   **Skin Store:** A marketplace to browse and purchase skins using earned CR.

## 5. Game Environments (Maps)
*   **Quantity:** 15 unique combat zones.
*   **Established Locations:**
    *   **Sector 7 Slums:** Dense urban warfare in a decaying metropolis (High Risk).
    *   **Orbital Nexus:** Zero-G zones and tight corridors (Medium Risk).
    *   **Tropic Outpost:** Open sightlines and heavy foliage (Low Risk).

## 6. Training & Practice
*   **Training Range:** A dedicated solo environment for skill development.
*   **Modes:**
    *   Target Practice (Static and moving targets).
    *   Bot Combat (AI-controlled opponents for live-fire drills).
*   **Analytics:** Real-time tracking of Accuracy (%), Targets Hit, and Bot Kills.

## 7. Visual Direction (Gunfight Tactical HUD)
*   **Aesthetic:** Sci-fi Cyberpunk / Tactical Military.
*   **Color Palette:** Dark-themed surface (#121316) with high-contrast Cyan (#00f0ff) and Neon Green accents.
*   **Typography:** Bold, industrial-styled 'Anybody' font for HUD clarity.
*   **UI Elements:** Dynamic radar mini-maps, holographic weapon readouts, and team status trackers.

## 8. Technical Architecture
*   **Language:** Localized in Traditional Chinese (ZH-TW).
*   **Enemy AI:** All opponents are computer-controlled (Bots).
*   **HUD Components:** Includes health bars, armor indicators, ammo counts, and a 15:00 countdown timer.
